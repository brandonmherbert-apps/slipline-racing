SLIPLINE RACING - PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Slipline Racing ("the game", "we", "us") handles
information on iPhone and iPad. The short version: the game does not collect,
sell or share your personal information. Gameplay progress syncs between your
own devices through your private iCloud account, and TestFlight reward
ownership does too.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

None. Slipline Racing has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking. The game does not send your gameplay or device
details to us. Apple services handle purchases, iCloud progress and Game
Center as described below.

We do not use your information to track you across other companies' apps or
websites, and the game does not ask for permission to do so.


2. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

To work, the game saves the following on your device:

  - Career progress, medals, records and best times
  - Ship unlocks, cosmetics and loadouts
  - Recorded runs used to show your best run ("ghosts")
  - Settings such as sound, controls and display options

Gameplay progress is saved on your device and can sync between your own
devices through your private iCloud key-value storage. This includes Career
and Elite progress, medals, records, Daily bests, XP, achievements and your
play streak. We cannot access this private iCloud data. Settings, loadouts and
ghost recordings stay on each device. Sync is tied to the iCloud account first
used by this save; switching accounts pauses it. Apple controls availability
and sync timing.

Deleting the game removes its local files but does not delete synced progress.
Manage synced data in Apple's iCloud storage settings. Reset Progress starts a
new progress history that your other devices on the same account adopt when
they sync. Purchases and Founding Pilot reward ownership are preserved. Local
files may also be included in your own device backups, handled by Apple under
its privacy policy.


3. TESTFLIGHT REWARD

If you play an eligible TestFlight build while signed in to iCloud, the game
stores a Founding Pilot ownership flag in your private iCloud key-value
storage. Apple syncs this flag to your devices using the same iCloud account,
allowing the exclusive paint and exhaust to return after reinstalling or
changing phones. The game keeps an account-specific ownership cache on your
device for offline use. We cannot access your private iCloud reward data.
Reset Progress does not erase this ownership. Deleting the app does not delete
the synced iCloud flag; you can manage the game's iCloud data in Apple's
iCloud storage settings. Apple controls sync timing, and deleting cloud data
may prevent restoration on another device.


4. PURCHASES

The game offers one optional in-app purchase that unlocks the full game.
Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never
receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The game
only receives confirmation from Apple that the full game has been unlocked,
so it can open the extra content and restore it if you reinstall.

Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/


5. GAME CENTER

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, the game sends achievement identifiers
and completion percentages, and your best scores for its leaderboards (Surge
stages and race and time trial times), to Apple. Other players can see
leaderboard scores with your Game Center name, as Game Center shows them.
Apple manages your Game Center account and profile under its privacy policy.
You can play offline without signing in. Resetting local progress does not
reset Game Center achievements or scores. We do not operate a server that
collects your gameplay progress.


6. SHARING YOUR BUILD

If you choose Share in the hangar, the game creates a poster on your device
showing your selected craft, paint, exhaust and personal records. The iOS share
sheet sends that image only to the app or destination you select. Slipline does
not upload the poster to a developer-operated server. Choosing Save Image may
ask permission to add the poster to Photos; the game does not read your library.


7. GAME CONTROLLERS

If you connect a game controller, the game reads its button and stick input
to let you play. This input is used only on your device and is not stored or
sent anywhere.


8. CRASH REPORTS AND FEEDBACK

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings,
Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You
control this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight and send
feedback, Apple passes us the feedback, any screenshots you attach, and the
basic device information Apple includes with it. We use this only to fix
problems and improve the game.

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to
reply to you, and we do not share them with anyone.


9. CHILDREN

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children under 13. Because the game collects no personal
information, there is nothing for a parent to request or delete from us.


10. THIRD PARTIES

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software.
The only outside services it uses are Apple's (the App Store, in-app
purchases, iCloud and Game Center), described above.


11. YOUR CHOICES

  - You can play without signing in to anything.
  - You can delete local game data by deleting the game; manage synced
    progress and reward data separately in iCloud storage settings.
  - You can turn off sharing analytics with developers in your device's
    Settings.


12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
with a new effective date. If the game ever starts collecting information,
this policy will be updated before that happens.
