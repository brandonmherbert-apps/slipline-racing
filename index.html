Circuits

Slipline Racing features eight circuits and four permanent Career tiers. Four circuits and Rookie Career are available for free, with the remaining circuits and Career tiers available through a one-time Core Game purchase.

Three ways to race

Compete against rivals, beat your personal best, or survive as the speed increases.

Race

Compete against seven rivals using items and weapons to fight for first place.

Surge

Keep racing as the speed steadily increases. Survive as long as you can before crashing.

Time Trial

Race against the clock, improve your racing lines, and beat your personal best.

Career and customization

Work your way through Career Mode, earn medals, and unlock new craft, paints, exhaust effects, and app icons.
