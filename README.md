## The game

Eight circuits, from city streets and a snowy pass to a tunnel under the sea and
a ring around a planet. Seven rivals on the grid. A Career that takes you from
Rookie to Champion, winning new craft, paints and exhausts along the way.

**Race.** Four laps against seven rivals, with rockets, missiles, mines and
turbos from the item pads.

**Surge.** No rivals and no finish line. You get faster every stage until you
crash.

**Time Trial.** Three laps alone against the clock and the ghost of your best
run.

A Daily Challenge and five missions arrive every day. Progress syncs between your
devices through iCloud, with Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Play with
touch or a game controller.

## Price

Free to download, with four circuits and the Rookie tier. One $2.99 purchase
unlocks the other four circuits and the rest of the Career. No subscription, and
craft and cosmetics are never sold.
