SLIPLINE RACING - SUPPORT

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email:

  slipline-racing@proton.me

Please include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. We aim to
reply within a few days.


COMMON QUESTIONS

Restoring the full game
  If you bought the full game and it shows as locked (for example after
  reinstalling or on a new device), open the store screen in the game and tap
  Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account
  you used to buy it.

Progress after reinstalling
  Gameplay progress can return through iCloud when you use the same iCloud
  account. Allow time for your devices to sync. Settings, loadouts and ghost
  recordings stay on each device; deleting the game removes those local files
  unless you restore a device backup that includes them.

Resetting progress
  Reset Progress starts fresh here and on your other devices when they sync
  through the same iCloud account. Purchases and Founding Pilot ownership stay.

Game controllers
  Connect a controller in your device's Bluetooth settings, then open the
  game. Controls are explained in Settings > Controls.

Refunds
  Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit
  https://reportaproblem.apple.com
